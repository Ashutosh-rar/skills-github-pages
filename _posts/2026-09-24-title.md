Launched by NASA in 1977, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 were built to explore the outer planets and eventually continued into interstellar space.

Decades later, they are still sending scientific data back to Earth from billions of kilometers away. They carry the famous Golden Record, a collection of sounds, images, and messages from Earth intended as a snapshot of humanity.

Two spacecraft. One tiny message from Earth. An extraordinary journey into the unknown. 🚀
